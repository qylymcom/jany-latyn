## How to read this file

- **Claims.** Each inscription lists what the sources say about it, field by field (found, finder, find spot, size, lines, signs, script, content, date, where it is kept, how it was studied, identity), with the ids of the sources in `db/sources.json`. Two claims on one field that disagree are both given.
- **Places.** Coordinates place the nearest settlement a source names, with a radius for how far the stone may be from it. Sites without coordinates belong to their region only.
- **Runes.** A transcribed line is one right-to-left paragraph tagged `otk-Orkh`, the RUNES-SPEC tag for genuine inscriptions. ⁚ U+205A is the two-dot divider and ⸱ U+2E31 the one-dot divider. Below it are the signs as glyph ids (`db/signs.json`): `?` marks a partly legible sign, `~` a sign drawn mirrored, and a grapheme id without a number a sign whose shape the source does not show. The reader output is the engine's inscription reader (RUNES-SPEC §7.2): one value per sign, no vowels supplied.
