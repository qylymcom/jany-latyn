The proverbs and the two tales were taken from the **Kyrgyz Folklore Text Corpus v1.0**
(<https://mozilladatacollective.com/datasets/cmlqoukmi000hnr07cprdmxsc>), which is released under
**CC0 1.0 Universal**. That waiver covers any purpose, including commercial use. The corpus digitized the
texts from the *El adabiyaty* series held by the Central Scientific Library of the National Academy
of Sciences of the Kyrgyz Republic. It contains only the folklore text itself; the compilers'
commentary is excluded. Separately, under Article 8 of the Law of the Kyrgyz Republic "On Copyright and Related
Rights", works of folklore and official texts are not subject to copyright. The corpus maintainers
ask for attribution, which this file provides.
