# Trademark and naming

The licenses in this repository cover copyright. They do not grant any rights
in the project's name, logo, or domain name.

"Jany-Latyn" and the project logo identify this project and the standard it
publishes. Please do not use them for a modified version, a fork, or a
different orthography, in a way that suggests it is this project or is endorsed
by it. This is the same reservation Apache 2.0 §6 makes, stated here because
the MIT license does not.

**You may, without asking:**

- Say that your software uses, implements, or is compatible with Jany-Latyn.
- Name the project when citing, reviewing, teaching, criticizing, or reporting
  on it.
- Keep the name in an unmodified copy of the code, along with its copyright
  notice.

**Please ask first before:**

- Naming a fork, a modified version, or a competing system "Jany-Latyn" or a
  confusingly similar variant.
- Using the logo as the mark of your own product or service.
- Registering a domain, package, app, or social account that would be mistaken
  for an official one.

A fork is welcome. Call it something of its own and say what it derives from —
for example, "an orthography derived from Jany-Latyn."
